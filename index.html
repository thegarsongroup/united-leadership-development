Why financial education matters

Small decisions, made with confidence, add up.

Most households are working toward the same four things. Knowing how each one works makes the next step easier to take.

Savings

A cash cushion turns a surprise bill into an inconvenience instead of a crisis.

Protection

Understanding coverage helps families plan for the people who depend on them.

Legacy

Planning ahead lets you decide what you leave behind and for whom.

Long-term growth

Time and consistency do much of the work when money is set aside early.

Your free introduction

What to expect, step by step.

No pressure and no jargon. The first meeting is about understanding where you are and what you want.

  1. STEP 1

    Join the waitlist

    Share your name, phone and email. It takes about a minute, and the extra questions are optional.

  2. STEP 2

    We reach out

    When a spot opens, our team contacts you to find a time for your free introduction at our Brooklyn Park office.

  3. STEP 3

    A real conversation

    Talk through your goals, your questions and what feels most pressing, whether that's saving, debt or protecting your family.

  4. STEP 4

    Leave with clarity

    Walk away understanding your options and the next steps you might take. What you do next is always your choice.

Spots are limited, so introductions are scheduled in the order requests come in.

Questions

Good to know before you sign up.

Is the introductory meeting really free?

Yes. The introduction is free and there is no obligation to do anything afterward.

Does joining the waitlist book an appointment?

No. Joining the waitlist is a request. Our team will contact you when a spot opens to find a time that works for you. Your meeting is confirmed only after you hear from us.

Where do meetings happen?

At our office: 7100 Northland Circle North, Suite #303, Brooklyn Park, MN 55428.

Do I have to share my income?

No. Income range is optional, and "Prefer not to say" is always fine. Only your name, phone number and email are required.

Is this financial advice?

The content on this site, including the calculators, is general education. It is not individualized financial, investment, insurance, legal or tax advice.

What happens to my information?

We use it only to contact you about your free introduction, and we don't sell it. Text messages are sent only if you check the separate SMS box. See the privacy policy at the bottom of this page.
